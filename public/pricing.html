Gas postcode → LDZ and exit zone

Full postcode, always. 348 of 3,100 national outcodes map to more than one exit zone depending on the incode, so an outcode-only answer is wrong for 11% of sites and looks entirely normal on a quote. If the full postcode is not held and the outcode is ambiguous, this returns nothing rather than a guess.

MPAN top line → TCR band and non-energy charges

Distributor id comes from the first two digits of the MPAN core. The LLFC carries the band: for distributors 10–23 it is the trailing digits, for independent networks 24–27 it is the last letter (F, G, H, J, K = no residual, 1, 2, 3, 4). Charges are the 2026/27 charging year and refresh annually.
