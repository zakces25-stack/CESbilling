Customer & settings

Factor year and green share are admin-only — the client portal cannot change them.

Carbon portal display — SBTi

Controls the trend graph on this customer's Overview page. Enrolled customers see the SBTi 1.5 °C pathway; everyone else sees a trend, projection and a target you set below.

The next-year target is used for the non-SBTi trend graph (a target line alongside the customer's projection). It's optional — leave blank to show trend + projection only.
